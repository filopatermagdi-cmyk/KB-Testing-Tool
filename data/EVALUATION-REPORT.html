4. Method notes

  • Arabic-native judging: Arabic answers are compared directly with their Arabic retrieval context – no translation step, eliminating translation-hallucination failures.
  • No claim truncation: explicit instruction to copy sentences verbatim fixed the long-list case (19 cities) for every judge.
  • Natural closings: phrases like “Any other service?” are treated as normal assistant behaviour, not penalized.
  • Auto-retry: transient failures (malformed JSON, empty completions, HTTP 429) are retried up to 3 times automatically.
  • Per-judge result files: switching judges can never overwrite another judge's scores.
